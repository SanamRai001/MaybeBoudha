## Why a permission gate is required

Boudhanath is one of the seven Monument Zones of the Kathmandu Valley UNESCO World Heritage property.

UNESCO states that the Kathmandu Valley property is protected nationally under Nepal's Ancient Monument Preservation Act and identifies Bauddhanath as one of its protected monument zones.

Source:

- https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/121

The Nepal Department of Archaeology (DoA) currently lists:

- photography work for historical buildings, palaces, archaeological sites, monasteries and temples under its Photography and Documentation Branch;
- consent for filming at ancient monuments;
- a Photography Unit responsibility to grant filming permission, according to rules, to domestic and foreign filming teams inside protected monument areas.

Sources:

- https://doa.gov.np/pages/817310553/
- https://doa.gov.np/pages/947812015/

A systematic 120–180 image reconstruction pass intended to create a derived public 3D representation is materially different from casual tourist photography. The official pages found do not explicitly classify Gaussian Splatting, photogrammetry, or 3D reconstruction.

Therefore MaybeBoudha must obtain a written determination before capture rather than guessing that casual-photo rules apply.

## Proposed capture to describe accurately

Do not send a vague request for "some photos."

The intended Phase 3C capture is:

- purpose: engineering/portfolio digital-heritage prototype;
- subject: one small exterior section of Boudhanath;
- capture type: still photographs for Gaussian Splat / 3D reconstruction;
- approximate image count: **120–180**;
- camera: handheld phone main rear camera;
- method: several overlapping walking passes;
- location: public/permitted ground positions only;
- equipment: no lighting rig, no large tripod footprint, no barriers;
- people: architecture is the subject; avoid deliberate capture of worshippers;
- raw imagery: private by default;
- derived output: cleaned 3D Gaussian Splat / source PLY;
- intended publication: potentially a public portfolio/web heritage experience;
- commercial use: state honestly if that changes later;
- drone: **none**;
- climbing/restricted access: **none**;
- first session scope: partial proof only, not the whole monument/plaza.

If any of these details change, the permission determination must be rechecked.

## Drone decision

**No drone in Phase 3C.**

CAAN's published standard UAS operating conditions state that an unmanned aircraft is not operated over a populated area, among other restrictions.

Boudhanath is a heavily populated and visited urban monument area, so the ground-first plan remains the project baseline.

Sources:

- https://caanepal.gov.np/functions/general-aviation/drones
- https://caanepal.gov.np/storage/app/media/drone/sep%2024%202022/UAS%20Requirement%20final.pdf

A future aerial plan would be a separate phase with aviation, heritage/site, and other required approvals. It is not a fallback if ground capture is incomplete.

## Evidence rule

A verbal "it should be fine" is not enough for the project record.

Acceptable evidence:

- official email reply;
- signed/stamped permission letter;
- official application/approval reference;
- written message from an identifiable authorized office account;
- written statement that no prior permission is required for the described activity.

Record:

- authority;
- contact;
- date;
- exact scope approved;
- conditions;
- fees;
- validity period/date;
- reference number if any;
- copy/location of the evidence.

Do not commit private correspondence or personal phone numbers beyond already-public official contact details to a public repository. The public provenance record should reference private evidence without exposing unnecessary personal data.

## Go / no-go gate

### GO only if

- DoA has provided a written determination for the described systematic capture;
- the Boudhanath Area Development Committee has provided a written determination for its site requirements;
- all required approvals/fees/conditions are satisfied;
- the allowed scope includes derived 3D reconstruction/publication as intended;
- capture date/time is compatible with any stated restrictions;
- the capture operator has the evidence available on the day.

### NO-GO if

- either authority has not answered and prior consent may be required;
- permission scope is ambiguous;
- publication rights/conditions are unclear;
- requested activity changed materially after approval;
- the site is unusually crowded or a ceremony/event makes capture disrespectful or impractical;
- staff/security requests that capture stop;
- the only way to obtain coverage would require an unapproved restricted/elevated/drone position.

A no-go day is not a project failure. It protects the heritage site and prevents unusable/unauthorized source data.
