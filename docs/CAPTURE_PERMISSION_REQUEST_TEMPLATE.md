# Boudhanath Capture Permission Request Template

Use this as the base request to the Department of Archaeology and the Shree Boudhanath Area Development Committee.

Adapt the recipient and any requested administrative details, but do not soften or hide the fact that the photographs will be used to create a derived 3D reconstruction.

---

## Subject

Request for guidance/permission — ground-based photographic 3D reconstruction study at Boudhanath

## Request

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am developing **MaybeBoudha**, a small digital-heritage/engineering project intended to create an interactive browser-based representation of Boudhanath.

Before collecting any source material, I would like to confirm the permission and site requirements for a **limited ground-based systematic photography session**.

For the first proof, I propose to photograph only one small, publicly accessible exterior section of Boudhanath using a handheld phone camera. The planned session would involve approximately **120–180 overlapping still photographs** taken during several short walking passes from normal public/permitted ground positions.

The photographs would be processed into a **3D Gaussian Splat / reconstruction**. Raw source photographs would be kept private by default. After cleanup and privacy review, the derived reconstruction may later be used in a public browser-based portfolio/digital-heritage experience.

The proposed first session would involve:

- no drone or aerial operation;
- no climbing or entry into restricted areas;
- no deliberate photography of worshippers as the subject;
- no lighting rig or obstruction of public movement;
- no full-site scan in this first session;
- no alteration or physical contact with the monument.

Could you please advise in writing:

1. whether prior permission or consent is required for this activity;
2. which application/process and documents apply, if permission is required;
3. whether any location or documentation fee applies;
4. whether there are restrictions on date/time, camera equipment, movement, or capture positions;
5. whether publication of the derived 3D reconstruction is permitted;
6. whether portfolio/non-commercial publication and commercial use have different requirements;
7. whether approval from any additional authority is required; and
8. whether a later, larger capture would require a separate application.

I will not begin the systematic capture until the applicable requirements are confirmed.

Thank you for your guidance.

Sincerely,

Sanam Rai  
MaybeBoudha project
